Our Story

A Family Tradition
in Every Slice

At Papa Presto Pizza, we believe great food starts with great ingredients and a whole lot of heart. Located in the heart of Uniondale, NY, we've been serving our community hand-tossed pizza, fresh pasta, and authentic Italian dishes that keep families coming back.

Every pie is made to order with our signature dough, slow-simmered tomato sauce, and the finest mozzarella. From classic cheese slices to our legendary Sicilian pies, we put pride into every dish that leaves our kitchen.
