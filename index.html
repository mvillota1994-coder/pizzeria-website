Our Story

A Family Tradition
on Front Street

Papa Presto Pizza is a family-owned pizzeria in the heart of Uniondale, NY. We've built our reputation one slice at a time — serving the neighborhood hand-tossed pies, hearty pasta dishes, and fresh Italian food that keeps families, workers, and students coming back day after day.

Every pie starts with our signature dough, slow-simmered tomato sauce, and the finest whole milk mozzarella. Whether you're grabbing a quick slice on your lunch break or feeding the whole family on a Friday night, we put the same pride into every dish.
